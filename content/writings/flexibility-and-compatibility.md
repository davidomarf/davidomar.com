Flexibility can be mistaken for Compatibility.

Flexibility is about adapting to different scenarios to those you're already
adapted to.
This can mean many things. In relationship, it means learning to adapt and turn
your current shape to that that compliments the shape of the other partner
involved.

This might look like compatibility (show drawing).

But it's causing the flexible partner to lose shape. And I don't think this is
inconsequential to that person. 

You're very explicitly forming your boundaries. Losing shape. And you might even
forget what that shape was supposed to look like. Flexibility should be
corresponded. And incompatibilities recognized. And addressed. It might be an
unimportant incompatibility. 

But it might also be one that spikes the partner. And that compromises their
wellbeing. (shape poking another shape, creating tension and pain on shape b).

So it's important to be flexible. But it's important to not forget your shape is
worth preserving too. That you deserve flexibility.

And most importantly, not to mistake compatibility with flexibility. 

Is this person compatible with me? Or am I just being too flexible and making
the hard work to make us fit each other. And also, are we compatible? Or is my
partner adapting flexibly to whatever my needs and offerings are? Do I want a
partner like that? 

Both sides of the questions are worth having. And both are worth taking seriously.
